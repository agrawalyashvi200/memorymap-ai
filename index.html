The process

Three steps, in this order

Each step only works because of the one before it — that's why it's numbered.

01 — SNAP

Photograph it once

Take a picture the moment you put an item down — straight from your phone camera. The AI looks at the frame: the item, the shelf, the objects around it.

02 — STORE

AI writes the memory

It generates a short description of the item and its surroundings, tags the room, and timestamps it. No manual notes needed.

03 — SEARCH

Ask, in plain words

Type "where's my passport?" and it matches your question against every memory to find the most recent, most likely spot.
